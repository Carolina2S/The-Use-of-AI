01 · AI as a tool

AI as a tool

Artificial intelligence is not a creator, but a highly responsive instrument waiting for human direction. While it accelerates your workflow and explores new possibilities, its raw output remains completely meaningless without your intent. True authorship belongs solely to the person who decides what is worth keeping.

02 · Mastering the Tool

Mastering the Tool

  1. 01

    Set your intention

    Before opening the tool, know what you want to build. The machine needs a human blueprint; without your initial vision, it only generates noise.

  2. 02

    Start a dialogue

    Don’t just ask for a finished product. Use the AI to test hypotheses, brainstorm different approaches, or explain complex technical concepts.

  3. 03

    Deconstruct the output

    Never accept the first answer. Break down the generated material, identify its structural flaws, and look for unexpected “happy accidents”.

  4. 04

    Curate relentlessly

    The true creative act is knowing what to discard. Cut the generic parts and refine the details until you can fully claim authorship of the result.

03 · Three stations

Three stations.
One method.

The same four steps, applied at each computer in the semicircle.

  • 01

    Sound

    Generate a hundred textures, then listen like an editor. Keep the one fragment with character and let the rest fall silent.

    Texture · Rhythm · Silence

  • 02

    Image

    Start from a real place and a real light. Let the machine propose variations, then frame what it would never have risked.

    Light · Frame · Place

  • 03

    Code

    Ask for a structure, then ask why. Read every line and rewrite what you can’t explain until it answers to your blueprint.

    Structure · Logic · Syntax
